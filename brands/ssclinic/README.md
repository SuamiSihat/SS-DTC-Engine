## Content modes (decision 2026-10-21)
SS Clinic videos run in one of two modes. Full rules: `content_modes` in brand.json and `skills/dtc-reels-editor/references/meta-policy.md` section 1a.

**Educational (default)**: a real SS Clinic doctor teaches, in organic posts.
- Identified on screen with a lower third (name and title). Uniform or plain clothes are both fine; clothing does not change the rules.
- Close with a neutral line such as "Jumpa doktor untuk pemeriksaan jika ada simptom".
- No line or end card that sends viewers to SS Clinic ("kita check dan rawat di SS Clinic", "Datang ke SS Clinic"), no booking link, price, offer or testimonial.

**KOL ad**: a paid clinic advertisement presented by a hired KOL, never by a real doctor.
- KKLIU approval for each posting before it runs; the number goes in the batch spec.
- The KOL talks about the premises and the visit, not treatment results or a doctor's skill.
- MAB illustrative statement on screen if anyone portrays a doctor or patient.
- No discounts, offers, before/after, superlatives or countdowns.

Why: MAB Policy and Decision (Services) 12/2007, amended 1/2025, does not allow doctors in clinic advertisements. MMC Guideline V2/2025 bars doctors from soliciting patients. Act 290 s.4A.
