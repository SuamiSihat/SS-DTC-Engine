## Ring colours
Dr. MIT Ring is the green (lime) ring. The blue ring is MENSS Ring, a separate brand with stricter rules (no performance or size promises). Never mix them in one batch or video.

Doctor-led product ads: MMC Guideline V2/2025 5.14 discourages doctors from endorsing health-related products. The 2026-10-16 brand decision stands, but the doctor and an adviser should confirm it (see `skills/dtc-reels-editor/references/meta-policy.md`).
