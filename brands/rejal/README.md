## Product and video rules
Machine-readable: `product_rules` in brand.json.

**Bawang Lanang + Madu Tualang** (honey + pearl garlic) & **Rejal Madu Tualang**
- Angles: origin of the wild Tualang honey (Apis dorsata) and bawang lanang, family recipe and tradition, how to enjoy it daily (1-2 teaspoons morning or night).
- Styles: Story, Faceless / b-roll (fits best), Clean. Real product, warm low-key light, dark stone or aged wood, slow pour and crunch close-ups.
- Not allowed: testosterone, energy, stamina, immunity, blood-flow/circulation, cardiometabolic, digestive or sexual-health claims; "organic" / "organik" claims; doctor endorsement (food brand, MMC 5.14); green colour cues.
- **Live-site copy caution (`rejal.co`)**: The live site currently contains phrases such as *"Natural Immune Defense / menguatkan sistem imun"*, *"Healthy Blood Flow / circulation"*, *"Cardiometabolic Support"*, *"Melancarkan Penghadaman"*, and *"madu organik"*. These are **BLOCK** or restricted terms under `brand.json` and the staff claims review — never copy them into ad scripts, hooks or captions.
